# Official sources and scope

Checked: 2026-09-09. Scope: PTE Academic. A check date is not a promise that the rules remain unchanged.

| Source | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| [Pearson: Speaking & Writing](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/speaking-writing/) | Task instructions, timing, SWT form, SGD and RTS |
| [Pearson: Reading](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/reading/) | Reading task variants and scoring notes |
| [Pearson: Listening](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/test-format/listening/) | WFD, listening blanks, and negative marking |
| [Pearson: 2025 updates](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-updates-2025/) | Updated Academic format and the two added tasks |
| [Home Affairs: English language requirements](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language) | Accepted tests, test dates, and visa-specific requirements |

Task priorities, minutes, item counts, and review routines in this pack are coaching suggestions. They are not official score weights or a forecast of questions. The task map covers every current question type, but the pack is not a full course or question bank. Use the current Pearson Score Guide linked from Pearson's task pages for scoring details; do not invent fixed percentage contributions.

The immigration snapshot applies to PTE Academic tests taken on or after 7 August 2025. For older results, consult the appropriate dated table. Always check the relevant visa or institution separately before deciding whether a score meets a requirement.
