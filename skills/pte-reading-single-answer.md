# PTE Reading | Multiple Choice, Single Answer

## Purpose

- Choose the one answer best supported by the passage.

## When to use

- When main idea, inference, or specific-detail questions repeatedly go wrong.

## Success looks like

- A choice tied to textual evidence and the exact question.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing an option because it repeats a word; treating plausible outside knowledge as evidence.

## Practice method

1. Read the question.
2. Locate relevant evidence and its context.
3. Eliminate options that distort scope, cause, or certainty.
4. Choose one and review the evidence afterwards.

## Special notes

- Practice distinction: “some students” does not support an answer about “all students”. Single-answer questions do not use the multiple-answer negative-marking rule.

## Daily target

- Brief timed practice and evidence review; adjust volume to errors.
