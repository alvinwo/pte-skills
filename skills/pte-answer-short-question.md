# PTE ASQ | Answer Short Question

## Purpose

- Understand a short spoken question and give a concise answer.

## When to use

- When familiar questions are misunderstood or the expected answer category is unclear.

## Success looks like

- A relevant word or short phrase without an unnecessary speech.

## Common mistakes

- Missing the question word; overexplaining; preparing a long sentence while time passes.

## Practice method

1. Listen for the whole question.
2. Identify whether it asks for a person, place, object, number, or action.
3. Give the shortest sufficient answer.
4. Review whether the error was vocabulary or listening.

## Special notes

- You have 10 seconds to answer. The microphone opens after the question without a tone: start when the status shows Recording. Waiting more than 3 seconds can close the recording.
- Original drill: What instrument measures temperature? Answer: a thermometer. Hide the answer during practice.

## Daily target

- A short familiarization or repair block; do not crowd out a larger demonstrated weakness.
