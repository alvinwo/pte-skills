# PTE HIW | Highlight Incorrect Words

## Purpose

- Identify displayed words that differ from the recording.

## When to use

- When text/audio tracking or uncertain clicking causes mistakes.

## Success looks like

- Only confirmed mismatches are selected; tracking continues after a miss.

## Common mistakes

- Clicking on weak pronunciation guesses; returning to a missed word and losing the audio.

## Practice method

1. Preview the text while the interface allows.
2. Track the recording across the displayed words.
3. Select confirmed mismatches.
4. If lost, rejoin at the next clear phrase and keep going.

## Special notes

- Wrong selections lose points. Original drill: text has “fifteen”, audio says “fifty”; select the displayed word “fifteen”. Use separate audio so the answer is not exposed.

## Daily target

- Short focused practice and review of mistaken clicks and lost positions.
