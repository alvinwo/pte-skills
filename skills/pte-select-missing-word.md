# PTE SMW | Select Missing Word

## Purpose

- Choose the ending that best completes the recording.

## When to use

- When the final idea or logical direction is missed.

## Success looks like

- The selected word or phrase fits both the meaning and the ending.

## Common mistakes

- Guessing from grammar alone; losing attention near the end.

## Practice method

1. Follow the topic and development.
2. Pay particular attention to the final clause before the signal.
3. Compare endings for meaning and grammatical fit.
4. Review the final audio segment after answering.

## Special notes

- The missing ending can be a word or group of words. Keep the whole message in mind, not only the last isolated word.

## Daily target

- Brief targeted practice within the listening budget.
