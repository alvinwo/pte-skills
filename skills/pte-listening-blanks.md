# PTE FIB-L | Fill in the Blanks (Type In)

## Purpose

- Type missing words into the displayed transcript while following the audio.

## When to use

- When tracking position, spelling, or word endings causes errors.

## Success looks like

- The heard word is entered accurately with its correct form.

## Common mistakes

- Stopping at one missed blank and losing the next; replacing the heard word with a synonym.

## Practice method

1. Preview the gaps and surrounding grammar.
2. Follow the audio and enter words or short recall cues.
3. Move on if one gap is missed.
4. Before advancing, check spelling and endings against what was heard.

## Special notes

- Do not substitute a synonym. Use rough cues only if you can reliably reconstruct the exact word.

## Daily target

- A short timed block plus spelling review.
