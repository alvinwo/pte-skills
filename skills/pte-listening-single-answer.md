# PTE Listening | Multiple Choice, Single Answer

## Purpose

- Choose one answer that matches the spoken message and question.

## When to use

- When detail, purpose, or inference is confused with a familiar keyword.

## Success looks like

- One supported answer with the speaker’s meaning preserved.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a repeated word despite a negative or correction later in the recording.

## Practice method

1. Preview the question and choices when available.
2. Listen for the requested information and changes of position.
3. Choose the best-supported answer.
4. Review the relevant audio segment afterwards.

## Special notes

- The audio plays once in the test. A practice replay is for review, not the initial attempt. Incorrect single selections do not invoke multiple-answer negative marking.

## Daily target

- A brief listening block without spending all the section budget on one item.
