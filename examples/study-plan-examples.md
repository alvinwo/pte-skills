These are examples, not assumed learner profiles. Confirm the test version, target and actual errors. Original examples below are practice material, not real exam questions.

## Example 1: Three weeks, 45 minutes daily, weak Writing

Targets and recent scores are unknown, so this is provisional.

| Block | Minutes | Activity |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Review / warm-up | 5 | Review spelling errors or establish today's focus |
| WFD | 15 | Attempt before seeing the transcript; classify omissions, spelling and word forms |
| SWT | 10 | One timed attempt; check sentence and word count |
| Sentence work and review | 10 | Correct the main SWT problem and retry |
| RS/RA maintenance and log | 5 | Brief practice and one next-review note |
| **Total** | **45** | Stop at the time limit |

If WE is the actual bottleneck, combine the SWT and sentence-work blocks into one 20-minute essay attempt and review it next session. Week 1 identifies recurring errors; week 2 adds unfamiliar and timed practice; week 3 adjusts to the evidence and allows rest. Readiness depends on observed performance.

## Original WFD feedback example — show after the attempt

Source: The students have submitted their final reports.

Learner: The student have submitted the final report.

| Learner | Source | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| student | students | Missing plural ending |
| the | their | Word substitution; replay to investigate |
| report | reports | Missing plural ending |

What worked: the learner retained “have submitted”. Focus this retry on plural endings: replay students/reports, hide the answer, and rewrite the sentence. Then try an unfamiliar sentence.

This is text comparison, not an official score. Without audio or process evidence, do not assume whether omissions arose in listening, memory or typing.
